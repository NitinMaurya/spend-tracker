Sequential & emphasis

The default. Category rankings, trends and utilisation are magnitude, not identity — one hue for every mark, or one accent against grey when a single figure is the story.

Why six slots became three

The shipped palette declares six hues but carries five: --s3 #1baf7a and --s6 #008300 are both greens, ΔE 1.6 apart under deuteranopia. --s6 also has no dark step — the same #008300 is declared in both modes. Every replacement sixth hue tested collides with an existing slot, and pink against orange fails the normal-vision floor on its own. Six mutually distinguishable hues do not exist inside this lightness band, so the palette stops claiming them.
